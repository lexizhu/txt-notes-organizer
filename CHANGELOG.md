## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-07

### Added

- Plain-text note capture with configurable blank-line separators
- Background macOS watcher and click-to-open launcher
- Local Ollama organization with validated classification, formatting, and table caches
- Optional standard OpenAI-compatible model transport
- Self-contained offline review HTML
- Project filters, hidden records, restoration, and permanent generated-data removal
- Previewed and confirmed one-time historical reprocessing
- Cooperative cancellation for daily organization and one-time historical reprocessing
- Dynamic cancellation wait estimates based on the active model timeout
- Fictional demo notes covering lists, dates, statuses, and tables
- Loopback-only local service with token, Host, and Origin protection
- Project-instance health identity to prevent reusing a service from a moved directory
